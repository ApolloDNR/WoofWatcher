# WoofWatcher Locked Business Plan And PRD

Date: 2026-06-12

## Product

WoofWatcher is a real-life digital pet care app for a dog and their household.

It is not a generic dog tracker. It is a care operating system that connects the real dog, the household, the care record, and a digital care twin.

## Positioning

- Core line: Real care. Pixel heart.
- Tagline: Your dog's day, brought to life.
- Promise: Care for your real dog. Watch their care twin come alive.

## Primary Users

- Main dog owners in a household.
- Sitters, walkers, trainers, and family helpers.
- Vet-facing use cases through exports and reports.

## Paid Value

- Household coordination.
- Advanced diet, meal, and health pattern organization.
- Records and credential vault.
- Care Pass exports for vets, sitters, trainers, and emergencies.
- WoofGuide owner-reviewed summaries, questions, drafts, and pattern explanations.
- Avatar/twin delight that makes real care feel emotionally rewarding.

## v1.5 Product Requirements

- Local-first care state remains reliable.
- Routines define expected care.
- Logs record actual care.
- Matching household-visible logs satisfy or update routines.
- Meal logs support expected portion, served amount, eaten amount, skipped/partial completion, notes, and household visibility.
- Potty is one parent action with pee/poop/both/tried/accident outcomes.
- Every major action must connect to useful care data or a clear destination.
- Health language must organize evidence without diagnosing.
- No fake live AI, payments, cloud sync, geolocation, or veterinary authority.

## Release Policy

- Checkout remains disabled until business, support, refund, privacy, and app-store obligations are approved.
- Provider-backed sync, document storage, push notifications, and AI actions must be truthful about readiness.
- v1.5 may use local mock state and static pixel assets where real providers are not implemented.
