# Money Release Plan

## Revenue Thesis

The fastest money path is paid consumer subscription. The premium wedge is not generic tracking. The premium wedge is shared care, health watch, household coordination, handoff, and vet/sitter reports.

WoofWatcher can become a meaningful niche subscription business if it becomes the trusted shared-care app for serious dog owners.

## Pricing Ladder

| Tier | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Free | One dog, basic logs, basic profile, limited history |
| Plus | $7-$10/month for advanced logs, reminders, health watch, reports |
| Family | $12-$15/month for multiple caregivers, roles, handoffs, shared calendar |
| Annual | $79-$149/year |
| Pro Reports / Sitter-Vet Pack | Add-on for exportable reports, stored report history, PDF packs |
| Later Partnerships | Vets, trainers, walkers, groomers, pet insurance |

## Do Not Monetize Yet

Do not add real payment flows until privacy terms, provider-backed account deletion, support obligations, launch target, subscription packaging, and refund/support workflow are clear. It is acceptable to add pricing surfaces or a gated premium preview before payment wiring.
