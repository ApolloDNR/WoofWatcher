# 30-Year North Star

WoofWatcher becomes the trusted lifetime operating system for dog care.

It is the shared source of truth for a dog's routines, health signals, records, caregivers, vet context, handoffs, behavior, training, medication, food, documents, and daily life.

## Mature Product

At maturity, WoofWatcher knows the dog's normal routine, diet, appetite patterns, health watch signals, medication schedule, care team, records, and handoff needs. It can explain what changed, what is due, what was missed, what needs follow-up, and what should be shown to a sitter, trainer, walker, or vet.

## Long-Term System Shape

- Dog profile as canonical identity and care baseline.
- Routines as expected care obligations.
- Logs as evidence of actual care.
- Health Watch as non-diagnostic pattern organization.
- Records as the trusted vault for vaccines, vet visits, insurance, microchip, receipts, and documents.
- Reports as owner-reviewed exports for sitters, family, trainers, and vets.
- WoofGuide as a bounded AI care copilot.
- Household permissions as the trust layer.
- Subscriptions, reports, and partnerships as the revenue layer.

## Experience Standard

The product should feel emotionally warm but not childish. It should earn trust through clarity, speed, useful defaults, visible sync state, clean handoffs, and real care outcomes.

The home screen should answer: What does my dog need now?

The app should make serious dog owners feel that everyone caring for the dog is finally working from the same source of truth.
