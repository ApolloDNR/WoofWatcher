## What changed

### Data trust and privacy

- Account and household identities now select distinct cache/recovery keys.
  An old account cannot seed the next account's care state.
- Identity changes, device wipes, and household switches invalidate delayed
  mutations before they can resurrect stale data.
- Device clearing returns a truthful per-target receipt. A partial failure is
  never presented as complete, and clearing a device does not claim to delete
  provider data.
- Private care entries and tombstones are filtered server-side. Only the
  author can read or mutate a private row; shared rows keep the role policy.
- Active-household selection is durable. Invitation acceptance is atomic,
  caller-scoped, and cannot be claimed twice.
- Care-document changes merge disjoint fields, while same-field conflicts
  remain owner-visible.
- Care-entry updates are revisioned and serialized; stale writers receive a
  typed conflict instead of silently overwriting newer care.
- Complete history pagination safely covers 251/501-row and equal-timestamp
  cases without replacing the local view with a partial snapshot.
- Walk-route recording is disclosed as precise foreground location. Route
  points follow the care entry's local/provider household boundary, and map
  viewing discloses the OpenStreetMap area request. Background location stays
  disabled.

Migrations `0004_care_entry_visibility.sql`,
`0005_user_active_household.sql`, `0006_care_entry_revision.sql`, and
`0008_care_entry_history_cursor.sql` are committed and database-tested. They
are **not** proven applied to an approved production project; migration,
backfill, RLS, retention, export, and deletion evidence remains provider-gated.

### Product and navigation

- Visible primary navigation is exactly Today, Plan, central Quick Log,
  Health, and More.
- Today is focused on the immediate care loop: Now, Next, Quick Log, real
  evidence, and Story progress earned from saved care.
- Plan owns routines, reminder candidates, responsibility, and schedule
  editing.
- Fast Log and detailed Log share one taxonomy/controller. Log opens on
  searchable History; the duplicate quick-care console was deleted.
- Health Watch uses owner-entered seven-day evidence and explicit
  not-logged/insufficient-evidence states. The unsupported simulated wellness
  score is gone.
- More is a grouped launch/household/tool directory, not a hidden second home.
- Zero-data states do not fabricate a dog, caregiver, care completion, health
  confidence, household readiness, or provider availability.

### Accessibility and interaction quality

- Core actions use shared 48-point targets and grow rather than
  shrink-to-fit.
- Today, Plan, Quick Log/Fast Log, detailed Log, Health, More, and shared
  board primitives reflow at accessibility sizes.
- Selectors expose role, selected/checked state, labels, and state-specific
  hints on native and web.
- Plan and More sheets are scrollable, keyboard-safe, and keep their final
  action reachable.
- Secondary routes retain explicit Back behavior; primary tabs retain the
  floating paw navigation.
- The web QA build supports an explicit, production-disabled font-scale probe
  so 1.0×/1.4×/2.0× layout branches can be rendered and captured. This is
  preflight evidence only; native Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and TalkBack remain
  device gates.

### Release honesty

- Local route/auth fallback is web-smoke-only and cannot activate in a native
  release.
- `smoke:runtime` rejects stale exports by comparing a SHA-256 fingerprint of
  the mobile/shared export inputs.
- Standalone `proof:live-preview` applies the same freshness check; the old
  July 3 recorded packet is explicitly historical.
- Cloud storage, live AI, push, payments, destructive account deletion,
  store accounts, and public launch remain blocked until their structured
  proof manifests are complete.
- The legacy local-only policies and store answers remain drafts, not approved
  disclosures. The release docs now block “Data Not Collected,” “no login,” or
  “everything stays on device” from being reused for a shared-account binary.
